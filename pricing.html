Common Questions
How long does a website take?

Most small business websites take a few weeks once the page list, content, and direction are clear. Timing depends on page count, revisions, and how quickly content is ready.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. I can review your existing site, keep what works, and rebuild the parts that are hurting clarity, credibility, mobile usability, or lead generation.

Do you help with SEO?

I include SEO foundations such as clean structure, metadata, headings, search-friendly page content, and local service messaging. Advanced SEO can be scoped into larger projects.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Ongoing support is available for content updates, maintenance, new pages, performance improvements, and small changes after launch.
